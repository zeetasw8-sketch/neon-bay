NEONBAY
An open-world crime story. Your city. Your rules.
Dex Marlowe came back to Neon Bay with nothing but a busted scooter and a phone number. The number belongs to Mara Voss — fixer, fence, and the only person in this city who pays on time. Six jobs stand between Dex and the big score. Steal cars, outrun the Bay PD, and don't get caught.
On foot: WASD to move, SHIFT to sprint, F to punch, CLICK to shoot (after the pistol job), drag mouse to look around.
In a car: walk up to any car and press E to take it. W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, SPACE handbrakes.
Heat: stealing, shooting and hitting people raises your wanted level. Lose the cops to cool off — or pay the price.
Missions: follow the gold beacon. Finish all six jobs to become a Neon Bay legend. Progress saves automatically.
A fully original game — own city, characters, vehicles, story, art and sound. No network needed. Best in desktop Chrome.
