NEON BAY

An open-world crime story at sunset. Six jobs. One city. No way back.

Dex Marlowe is back in Neon Bay, broke and out of options. His fixer Mara Voss has six jobs — a pickup, a hot delivery, a stolen car, a heat problem, a dock fight, and the big one at Meridian Bank. Do them clean, get paid, and maybe the city lets you keep the money.

HOW TO PLAY

Move / drive: WASD  ·  Sprint: SHIFT  ·  Enter / exit car: E  ·  Punch: F  ·  Shoot (once you have the pistol): CLICK  ·  Handbrake: SPACE  ·  Retry mission: R  ·  Pause: ESC / P  ·  Mute: M  ·  Look: drag mouse  ·  Zoom: wheel

Wanted: stealing a car, punching or shooting a bystander raises your heat. Police chase you; if one stays close while you are slow for 2 seconds, you are BUSTED. Get over 45 m from every cop car and stay clean — a star fades every 20 seconds.

Minimap: the gold ▲ is you, the white ● with a gold ring is your objective (it clamps to the edge when far away — follow the gold triangle), the blue ■ is the Bank, the red + is the Hospital, the green ⌂ is your Garage. Follow the gold beacon in the sky to reach your objective.

Jobs: 1 First Pickup $200 · 2 Hot Delivery $350 (100 s) · 3 Borrowed Wheels $400 · 4 Lose the Heat $500 · 5 Dockside Trouble $600 · 6 The Big One $1,500. Finish all six to become a Neon Bay Legend.
