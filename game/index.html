NEON BAY

Six jobs. One city. Three stars of heat.

You are Dex Marlowe, a driver fresh out of luck. Your fixer Mara Voss has six jobs for you, from a quiet pickup at the pier to the big one at the bank. Steal the case, lose the cops, get home.

An original open-world crime game. Three.js. No network needed.

HOW TO PLAY

Move — WASD / arrows. Sprint — hold SHIFT. Camera — drag the mouse, wheel zooms.

Enter / exit a car — E near a car (stealing one raises wanted by 1★, except your own car by the garage).

Punch — F. Shoot — click, once you have the pistol (Job 5). A shot that misses raises no heat.

Wanted — 0 to 5 stars. One police car chases per star. Stay slow and close to a police car for 2 seconds and you get busted. Lay low and the heat fades, one star at a time.

Jobs — follow the gold beacon and the ⬆ arrow. Six jobs from Mara Voss, $3,550 in total. Finish all six to become a Neon Bay Legend.

Retry — R restarts the current job at the garage. Pause — P or ESC. Mute — M. The game saves automatically.

Minimap — ▲ is you, ● yellow is traffic, ● red is police, ◎ gold-ringed is the objective, ■ green the garage, ■ gold the bank, ■ white the hospital. N is north.
