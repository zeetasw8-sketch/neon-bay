NEON BAY

Six jobs. One city. No way out but through.

You are Dex Marlowe, a driver fresh out of a bad deal in Neon Bay — a purple-dusk port city of five-by-five blocks, a beach, and a police force that never sleeps. Every car in this city belongs to someone else — taking one raises your wanted level. Mara Voss has six jobs for you. Finish them all and the city is yours.

WASD — walk / drive  ·  SHIFT — sprint  ·  SPACE — handbrake

E — enter / exit car (stealing a car raises your wanted level)

F — punch  ·  CLICK — shoot (only with a pistol)

Drag mouse to look around, wheel to zoom the camera.

P / ESC — pause  ·  R — retry a failed job  ·  M — mute

Follow the gold beacon. Lose the cops by getting more than 45 m away from every patrol car — your wanted level drops one star every 20 seconds.

WASTED or BUSTED costs a $150 fine. Your progress saves after every job.

A fan-made open-world crime game. Not affiliated with any commercial game.
