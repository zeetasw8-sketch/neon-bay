NEON BAY

An open-world crime game. Sunset over the bay, six jobs, one fixer, and the Bay PD on your tail.

You are Dex Marlowe, a wheelman with a teal jacket and a pink starter car. Mara Voss has six jobs for you — from a first pickup at the south pier to the big bank job. Steal cars, lose the heat, fight at the docks, and get out clean.

A completely original game. No real city, no real people, no borrowed art, music or names.

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: finish all 6 jobs for Mara Voss, then roam free. Each job pays cash.

Move: WASD — walk and drive. E enter / exit a car (entering a car is stealing: +1 wanted star). F punch. Mouse click shoot once you have the pistol. SHIFT sprint. SPACE handbrake. R retry the current job. P or ESC pause. M mute.

Camera: drag the mouse to look around; the wheel zooms. The camera follows you and swings back behind you as you move.

Wanted: stars rise when you steal a car, punch a pedestrian, or hit one with a car. One police car hunts you per star. Police bust you if one stays within 5 m while you move slower than 3 m/s for 2 seconds. To lose the heat, get more than 45 m away from every police car and stay clean — stars fade one per 20 seconds.

Minimap: the round map shows the city, the sea to the south, your garage (home), the bank, the hospital, traffic and police. The white dot with a gold ring is your objective; the gold arrow and the distance above the map point at it too. The gold beam in the sky marks the objective from anywhere in the city.

Health: you heal fully at the start of every job. If you are wasted you pay a $150 fine and wake up at the hospital. If you are busted you pay the same fine and the job restarts.

Save: your cash and job are saved automatically. CONTINUE resumes them.
